## The contest points you are proving

- **Urgent human need:** Community-serving teams need useful capacity now.
- **Technical excellence:** Strands Agents, Amazon Bedrock, typed tools, deterministic server authority, action-bound approval, PostgreSQL, fresh-read verification, and durable audit history.
- **Working product:** The demo visibly produces `EXECUTE`, `APPROVAL REQUIRED`, verification failure, authority demotion, a changed future decision, and `BLOCKED`.
- **Originality:** Verified behavior changes actual capability—not merely a confidence score or dashboard badge.
- **Responsible AI for good:** Routine work moves to AI while consequential decisions and hard boundaries remain with people.
