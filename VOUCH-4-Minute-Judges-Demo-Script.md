## Contest points this presentation proves

- **Impact:** Community-serving teams gain capacity while people retain consequential decisions.

- **Originality:** Verified behavior changes actual capability—not merely a confidence score.
- **Working product:** The live flow proves autonomous execution, human authorization, verification failure, authority reduction, a changed future decision, and a hard block.
- **Responsible AI for good:** Useful autonomy grows through evidence while human responsibility and safety boundaries remain intact.
