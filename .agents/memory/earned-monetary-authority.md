---
name: Earned monetary authority
description: Deterministic rules connecting verified outcomes to the agent's persisted autonomous dollar limit.
---

The agent's autonomous dollar limit is server-held and evaluated separately from its reliability score and T1–T4 tier. A qualifying autonomously completed, independently verified action raises the limit by $250 up to $1,000. A failed verification reduces the limit to 20% of its prior value, with a $100 floor. Human-authorized work may improve reliability but does not expand the autonomous dollar limit.

**Why:** The product must prove that verified work changes future capability without allowing human approval or historical trust to become a permanent permission grant.

**How to apply:** Compare every monetary action with the persisted limit during fresh server authorization. Keep conflicts, unsafe evidence, policy thresholds, risk, and irreversibility authoritative even when the amount is within the earned limit.
