The goal is to make one conceptual correction:

VOUCH IS ABOUT TRUST — NOT KEYS

The current Good Neighbor positioning is substantially correct, but some language still frames VOUCH around "keys," access, or control.

That is NOT the fundamental idea.

The fundamental idea is:

People should be able to trust an AI teammate before giving it more responsibility.

VOUCH operationalizes that trust.

The agent proves itself.

Its work is verified.

Successful work can earn greater responsibility.

When something is uncertain or consequential, a person can step in.

When the evidence says the agent should no longer have the same level of responsibility, that responsibility can decrease.

The central concept is:

TRUST IS EARNED THROUGH PROVEN WORK.

THE CORE STORY

Use this mental model throughout the entire product:

THE PEOPLE

A group is trying to help other people.

They have limited time, attention, and capacity.

THE AI TEAMMATE

They bring in an AI agent to take on some of the repetitive work.

THE PROBLEM

The group cannot blindly trust the agent with increasing responsibility.

But they also cannot afford to personally approve every routine task.

VOUCH

VOUCH gives the group a way to build trust in the AI teammate through evidence and verified performance.

The agent starts with limited responsibility.

It proves what it can do.

The work is verified.

Trust can increase.

Risky decisions can come back to people.

Failed performance can reduce responsibility.

THE BENEFIT

The group gets more capacity to focus on the people they are actually trying to help.

That is the Good Neighbor story.
