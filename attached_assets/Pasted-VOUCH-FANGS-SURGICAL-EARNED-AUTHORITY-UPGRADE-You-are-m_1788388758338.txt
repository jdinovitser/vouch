9. DO NOT CONFUSE TRUST WITH AUTHORITY

Keep these concepts logically distinct.

Trust/reliability is evidence about the agent's performance.

Authority is what the agent is currently permitted to do.

The relationship should be deterministic and explainable.

Conceptually:

Verified history
→ Reliability / trust
→ Earned capability
→ Current authorization decision

The current request must STILL be evaluated using:

Do not allow past success to bypass current policy or safety boundaries.

This is critical.

Earned authority is bounded and revocable, not permanent permission.

10. CURRENT EVIDENCE MUST ALWAYS WIN

good history + low risk + valid evidence + policy compliant + authority sufficient = potentially ALLOW

But:

hard safety violation = BLOCK

regardless of historical performance.

This preserves the core VOUCH safety model.
