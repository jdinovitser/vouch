VOUCH — MAJOR PRODUCT, UX, BRAND & DEMO UPGRADE
Turn the working prototype into an exceptional modern SaaS product
The existing VOUCH application is functional and the core deterministic safety architecture has already been implemented and tested.
DO NOT rebuild the application from scratch.
DO NOT throw away the existing state machine, trust engine, authority engine, evidence model, risk engine, verification logic, session handling, audit system, or passing tests.
This task is a major product-design and demo-experience upgrade on top of the existing working application.
The goal is to transform VOUCH from a good hackathon prototype into a premium, modern SaaS product that immediately communicates business value, technical sophistication, and ROI.
The application should feel like a real company/product that could be shown to:
•	AWS engineers
•	enterprise technology leaders
•	CIOs
•	CTOs
•	security leaders
•	operations leaders
•	potential customers
•	hackathon judges
The experience must communicate:
VOUCH lets organizations safely give AI agents more autonomy without giving them unlimited authority.

1. CORE PRODUCT POSITIONING
Keep the brand:
VOUCH
Primary headline:
AI can act. VOUCH makes it earn the right.
Primary supporting statement:
Give AI agents the autonomy to move fast—with the controls to know when they should stop.
Core product concept:
VOUCH continuously evaluates:
Evidence → Risk → Authority → Action → Verification → Trust
The important business problem is:
Organizations want AI agents to perform real work.
But unrestricted autonomy creates risk.
Too little autonomy:
•	humans remain bottlenecks
•	agents cannot deliver meaningful operational value
•	automation becomes glorified assistance
Too much autonomy:
•	mistakes become actions
•	bad evidence becomes bad decisions
•	compromised instructions can become real-world consequences
•	failures can propagate automatically
VOUCH is the control layer between those extremes.
