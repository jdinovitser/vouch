VOUCH — MISSION-DRIVEN DEMO STORY PASS

We are making ONE focused improvement to the existing VOUCH application.

GOAL

The VOUCH demo mechanics are already working well.

Do NOT rebuild the demos. Do NOT change the underlying logic.

The goal of this pass is to make the existing demo cases clearly communicate the Good Neighbor mission:

Small community teams are trying to help people with limited time, people, and resources. An AI teammate can give them another set of hands—but that teammate needs to earn trust before taking on more responsibility.

The existing claims/case workflow is the technical demonstration of that idea.

We want visitors and hackathon judges to understand the human purpose first, while still seeing the exact same working VOUCH trust/authority/verification mechanics underneath.

The AWS Agents for Humans hackathon's Good Neighbor track is specifically for agents that help groups of people such as nonprofits, food banks, schools, libraries, neighborhoods, and small local organizations.

Do not pretend VOUCH is literally a food pantry software product.
