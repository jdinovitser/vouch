The product is NOT primarily:

“Adaptive authorization infrastructure for AI agents.”

The product is:

VOUCH is a claims resolution agent that safely resolves routine cases, escalates exceptions, verifies every outcome, and earns the right to handle more work over time.

Primary headline:

Resolve routine claims. Escalate only what needs judgment.

The agent recommends. VOUCH authorizes.

And:

Trust can expand capability, but trust never overrides current policy, evidence, risk, or context.
