# 1. THE CORE STORY

The entire demo should follow this narrative:

### PEOPLE NEED HELP

Small community teams have limited time and resources.

### AI CAN HELP

Modern AI agents can reason, inspect evidence, use tools, and perform real work.

### BUT TRUST MATTERS

If an AI is going to act on behalf of people, the team needs to know when it has earned that responsibility.

### THAT IS VOUCH

VOUCH is the trusted AI teammate.

It lets the AI:

**START SMALL → PROVE IT → EARN TRUST → TAKE ON MORE → KNOW WHEN TO STOP**

The agent does not receive unlimited authority.

It earns responsibility through verified results.

When the task is too risky, outside its earned authority, or violates a hard boundary, VOUCH puts the decision back in human hands.
