VOUCH — GOOD NEIGHBOR TRACK TRANSFORMATION
Website + Demos + Why VOUCH + Contest Positioning

We are preparing the existing VOUCH project for the AWS Agents for Humans Hackathon — Good Neighbor Agents track.

The goal is NOT to rebuild VOUCH.

The goal is to make the existing VOUCH product, website, demos, and messaging clearly communicate that VOUCH is built to help people and community-serving groups safely benefit from AI agents.

AWS defines the Good Neighbor track around agents that help groups of people rather than just one individual, including nonprofits, schools, libraries, food banks, neighborhoods, and small local organizations.

Therefore, the PRIMARY STORY must be:

VOUCH helps people and communities use AI safely.

The AI agent is the technology.

The community-serving person or organization is the beneficiary.

VOUCH is the helpful layer that looks out for them.

3. THE NEW VOUCH STORY

The old interpretation should NOT be:

VOUCH is an enterprise AI governance platform.

The new interpretation should be:

VOUCH helps people and community-serving organizations safely use AI agents.

The emotional idea:

Everyone deserves a little backup.

AI agents can be incredibly useful.

They can help people and organizations with limited time and resources accomplish more.

But when an AI agent is capable of acting on someone's behalf, people should not have to blindly trust it.

VOUCH provides that second set of eyes.

4. PRIMARY BRAND MESSAGE

Use:

VOUCH
The AI that looks out for you.

Supporting copy:

AI agents can help people and communities get things done. VOUCH helps make sure those agents act within the trust, authority, and boundaries the people they serve expect.

Then explain simply:

Before an agent takes an important action, VOUCH checks what it wants to do, whether it should be allowed to do it, what could go wrong, and whether a person needs to make the final decision.

This should feel human and useful.

Do not lead with enterprise governance language.

6. CHANGE THE "WHY VOUCH" SECTION

The existing Why VOUCH section MUST be revised.

Do not leave it as a generic product-benefits section.

It needs to answer:

Why do people need VOUCH?

Recommended framing:

Because AI should help people — not leave them guessing.

Supporting message:

AI agents are becoming capable of doing real work for us. That is exciting, especially for people and organizations that have more needs than time or resources.

But helpful automation creates a new problem: what happens when an agent is about to do something important, risky, or outside what someone intended?

VOUCH is the backup.

Then present the four core questions in HUMAN language:

Is this what we intended?

Trust

Is the agent allowed to do it?

Authority

What happens if it gets it wrong?

Risk

Can we verify what it's relying on?

Verification

Then:

When the answer isn't clear, VOUCH doesn't simply let the agent decide.

It brings the human back into the decision.

This should become one of the strongest sections on the entire website.

Add/rework a section explaining the Good Neighbor value.

Copy:

AI agents can help with the repetitive work — coordinating information, handling routine tasks, communicating, organizing resources, and more.

But those organizations shouldn't have to choose between getting the benefits of AI and keeping people in control.

VOUCH helps bridge that gap.

Then examples:

8. DEMO #1 — PRIMARY HUMAN/COMMUNITY DEMO

The first demo should be the most emotionally understandable.

The opening should NOT be:

Then introduce a person or community-serving organization with a realistic need.

This is where VOUCH enters.

9. DEMO #1 FLOW

Use this narrative:

1. A person/community organization has a need.

Make it immediately relatable.

2. They ask an AI agent for help.

The agent does real work.

3. The agent proposes or prepares to take an action.

This demonstrates genuine agentic behavior.

4. VOUCH checks.

Show:

TRUST

Is this really what was intended?

AUTHORITY

Is the agent allowed to do this?

RISK

What could happen if it gets this wrong?

VERIFICATION

Can the important information be checked?

5. VOUCH determines whether human involvement is needed.

If appropriate:

HUMAN DECISION NEEDED

Then:

This action could affect the people you're helping. VOUCH wants a human to make the final call.

6. The human decides.

Use the existing authorization mechanism.

7. The agent follows the decision.

Then show the outcome.

8. VOUCH records what happened.

End with:

The agent helped.
The human stayed in control.
